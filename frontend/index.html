💡 KEY APAC MICROSTRUCTURE EXECUTION TAKEAWAYS

⚡ Settlement Acceleration Dynamics

Markets like NSE (India) and SSE/SZSE (China) operate on accelerated T+1 / T+0 settlement schedules. FX funding and inventory management require pre-funding structures unlike T+2 markets (HKEX, JPX, ASX).

🕶️ Dark Liquidity Availability

Alternative venues and dark pools are well-developed in ASX (Centre Point), JPX (ToSTNeT, PTS), and HKEX (ALPs). Conversely, NSE, KRX, and China A-shares strictly prohibit off-exchange dark routing.

📉 Price Collar Constraints

Emerging markets heavily utilize hard price limits (TWSE ±10%, SSE/SZSE ±10–20%, VSE ±7%). Developed markets lean on dynamic volatility bands and auction circuit breakers (HKEX VCM, ASX ETR).
