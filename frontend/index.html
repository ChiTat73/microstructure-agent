⚙️ KEY APAC STRUCTURAL DIFFERENCES & MICROSTRUCTURE

🌊 Retail vs. Institutional Flow

Taiwan (~55%) and India (~38%) feature heavy retail flow leading to intra-day order imbalances. In contrast, HKEX and JPX are primarily driven by algorithmic foreign institutional order flow.

⏱️ Settlement Velocity

India leads APAC with operational T+1 and instant T+0 settlement modes, whereas cross-border hubs like HKEX and TSE maintain T+2 to accommodate global multi-currency clearing workflows.

🛡️ Circuit Breakers & Risk

TWSE enforces strict hard limits (±10%), which can lead to liquidity lockups during high-volatility events, while HKEX and NSE utilize dynamic price bands to maintain continuous price discovery.
