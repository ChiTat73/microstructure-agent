⚙️ KEY APAC STRUCTURAL DIFFERENCES & MICROSTRUCTURE INSIGHTS

🌊 Retail vs. Institutional Execution Dynamics

Retail dominance in Vietnam (88%), China (~75-82%), and South Korea (65%) introduces significant intraday momentum sweeps and order imbalances. Conversely, HKEX, JPX, and ASX feature heavy international algorithmic and dark pool execution.

⏱️ Accelerated Settlement Velocity

India leads the region with operational T+1 and instantaneous T+0 rolling settlements. China operates a unique hybrid (T+0 for equity delivery, T+1 for cash), while ASEAN and Australasia mostly maintain standard T+2 cycles.

🛡️ Circuit Breakers & Liquidity Lockups

Strict hard price limits in Taiwan (±10%), China (±10%), and Vietnam (±7%) regularly cause limit-up/limit-down trading freezes. HKEX (VCM) and NSE (Dynamic Bands) utilize brief cooling periods to allow continuous price discovery.
